# Dynamics 365 Licensing — Learning Knowledge Base

A structured, **fully-cited** knowledge base for learning everything about
**Microsoft Dynamics 365 licensing**, built **exclusively from official Microsoft
sources** — the *Dynamics 365 Licensing Guide (July 2026)* and *Microsoft Learn*.
Every factual claim carries an inline citation, so the material is traceable and
free of guesswork, and can be compiled directly into a comprehensive report.

- **Per-application** — Sales, Customer Service, Contact Center, Field Service, Finance, Supply Chain Management, Commerce, Human Resources, Project Operations, Business Central, Customer Insights, and specialized/add-on apps

- **Reference** — [`GLOSSARY.md`](./docs/GLOSSARY.md) and the [`SOURCES.md`](./docs/SOURCES.md) registry

## Sourcing policy

- **Official Microsoft only.** `learn.microsoft.com`, `www.microsoft.com`
  (Product Terms, licensing docs, pricing), the Licensing Guide PDF, and related
  Microsoft domains. No third-party blogs, resellers, or aggregators.
- **No hallucination.** Anything that could not be confirmed in an approved
  source was omitted or explicitly flagged, never invented.
- **Prices are not reproduced** (they change constantly); each file links to the
  official pricing page instead.

## Currency

- Based on the **Dynamics 365 Licensing Guide, July 2026 edition**. Guide page
  citations appear as `[LG p.N]`.
- Microsoft refreshes the guide monthly and changes pricing often — re-verify
  capacity numbers and offer names against the
  [latest guide](https://aka.ms/dynamicslicensingguide) and the
  [Product Terms](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms) before relying on
